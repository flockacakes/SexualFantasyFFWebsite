The Good, the Bad & the 0-5

Five weeks in, Sexual Fantasy has two teams standing at 4-1, a points-scoring machine lurking at 3-2, and an 0-5 roster that might want to consider filing a formal complaint against its schedule. It's a perfectly normal, extremely unserious fantasy football season.

Arturo's to infinity and bijan😞 owns first place at 4-1 with 661.64 points scored. Jake's Gibbs to the Crib is also 4-1. But Connor's Allen Wrench has accumulated 713.00 points, a league high, yet somehow sits 3-2. Apparently touchdowns and victories are separate subscriptions.

BY THE NUMBERS

151.60: Week's top score (Allen Wrench) · 67.74: Lowest score (Taylor Swift) · 5.12: Closest victory (Picken Cottons) · 0-5: Steeler Virginity's record.

The Trade Desk: A Blockbuster in Plain Sight

On October 8, Gibbs to the Crib acquired CeeDee Lamb, Tony Pollard, and Jaylen Wright from Steeler Virginity. Going the other way: George Pickens, Jordan Mason, Aaron Jones Sr., and Bucky Irving.

That's seven players in a trade between a current 4-1 contender and the league's last-place team. The deal may pay off for either side, but the timing guarantees scrutiny. Jake won Week 5; Spencer still hasn't found his first victory.
