Spencer Finally Scores. Arturo Has to Live With It.

Ten weeks. Ten losses. An entire fantasy season spent making the win column look like an unopened condom. Then Arturo showed up with 88.46 points and became the answer to a trivia question nobody wants attached to his name.

Steeler Virginity has officially been lost. Spencer is 1-10, Arturo is the first donor, and the league's safest weekly prediction just became evidence against this publication. Last week's pick was Arturo. Consider the editorial department properly fucked.

Meanwhile, first place changed hands again. Jake beat Sam, Connor removed Alex from the penthouse, and Nick delivered the week's only 140-point performance. The top six now consist of two 8-3 teams and four at 7-4, while the nearest challengers sit two wins behind the cutoff.

Spencer finally escaped zero. Everyone else is running out of room for one.

Their official records? Jack is 7-4. Cooper is 4-7.
