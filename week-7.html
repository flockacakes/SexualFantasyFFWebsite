Two Heartbreaks, One Undefeated Losing Streak, and a League That Refuses to Behave

Seven weeks in, Jake owns the best record, Sam owns the power rankings, and Connor owns the league's most points. Naturally, those are three different teams. Welcome to Sexual Fantasy, where being good and being rewarded for it remain separate hobbies.

The week's defining moments were measured in decimals: Connor escaped Hunter by 0.32 points, while Alex survived Nick by 1.24. Meanwhile, Spencer reached 0–7 despite finally posting a respectable 114.28. The football gods aren't merely ignoring his calls. They've blocked his number.

The Week's Biggest Headlines

Connor Wins by 0.32, Somehow

Allen Wrench 102.42, I Bite Women 102.10. Connor scored well below his season average and still took the win. Hunter came within one decent catch of changing the outcome. Connor now sits 4–3 with a league-leading 917.92 points scored. His record looks ordinary; his scoring résumé absolutely does not.

Alex Steals the Thriller of the Week

Life of a JoeGirl 129.52, Marvin’s Room 128.28. Nick put up the second-highest losing score of Week 7 and got nothing for it. Alex's Jaxon Smith-Njigba delivered 27.8, while Quinshon Judkins contributed 22.9. Nick countered with Dak Prescott's 29.28, Luther Burden III's 23.2, and Trey McBride's 23.1. The matchup deserved two winners. Yahoo issued one.

Jake Keeps Winning; Sam Keeps Scoring

Jake moved to 6–1 with a 34.22-point victory over Mason, powered by Tony Pollard's 36.0. Sam beat Jack by 35.12 and now leads the league in total points among the 5–2 teams with 873.88. Jake has the better record; Sam has the louder scoreboard.

Spencer's Losing Streak Develops a Personality

Steeler Virginity scored 114.28, its strongest showing in several weeks, and lost to Cooper's 122.36. At 0–7, Spencer is no longer simply waiting for a bounce-back. He's collecting increasingly elaborate evidence that the universe has a personal objection.

Winners and Losers

Winner: Alex. A 129.52-point performance, a 1.24-point win, and a five-place power-ranking leap. That's a productive week.

Winner: Rusty. Taylor Swift beat Arturo by 26.94 points and climbed three spots in the power rankings.

Loser: Nick. Scoring 128.28 and losing is fantasy football's version of doing everything right on the exam and discovering there was a group-project grade.

Loser: Jack. A 74.82-point week was the league's lowest of Week 7. The Panthers defense managed to subtract from the total.

Stat of the Week

0.32 points. That's all that separated Connor and Hunter. In a league where a passing yard is worth 0.04 points, eight passing yards are enough to explain the entire margin.

The Numbers Don't Lie

Connor has scored 917.92 points, the most in the league, but is only 4–3. His expected wins are approximately 4.91, almost one more than his actual record. That is a contender wearing the record of a middle-of-the-pack team.

Jake's 6–1 record comes with approximately 3.64 expected wins. That's a +2.36-win schedule-luck gap, the kind of cushion that makes everyone else suspicious of the schedule maker.

Spencer's 0–7 is harsh even relative to his 1.64 expected wins. The scoring has been weak, but a winless record also requires a particularly unforgiving run of opponents.

Sam's 5–2 and 4.55 expected wins tell a much more straightforward story: a strong team winning roughly as often as its weekly scores suggest.

Expected wins compare each weekly score against all 11 other teams; tied comparisons count as half a win. These are analytical estimates, not official results.

The Playoff Picture

Six teams will make the playoffs, beginning in Week 15. Jake leads at 6–1; Sam and Alex follow at 5–2. Connor, Arturo, Rusty and Jack are packed together at 4–3, with only six postseason spots available.

Nick and Mason are each 3–4, one game behind that crowded group. Hunter and Cooper are 2–5, while Spencer is still searching for win number one. No team has been declared mathematically clinched or eliminated.
